# Quick Start Guide

## 1. Sign in

Use your own email/password and complete verification. The Business Owner accepts the current business terms once per required revision; staff do not need to accept the commercial agreement individually. Forgot password requests administrator assistance.

## 2. Make a sale

In POS, add quick/custom or enabled inventory items. Check quantities and prices. Select payment, then complete once. Cash must cover the total; use Udhar for an unpaid balance. Print the saved receipt if needed.

## 3. Create a repair

Record the customer's contact details, device, problem, initial components/work and price. Record any advance actually received; it cannot exceed the total. Save the ticket and give the customer their reference.

## 4. Handle extra work

Workshop proposes extra work → Pending → customer decision → Approved additional invoice or Declined. Do not charge Pending/Declined work as though it were approved. Approved work stays linked to the original repair.

## 5. Take payments

Record full/partial payments or authorized credit through the existing payment actions. Repair-family payments go to the parent first, then older additional invoices. Later payment does not create another billable invoice.

## 6. Work the job

Workshop reviews Pending jobs, moves work to In Progress and records relevant operational notes. Technician financial summaries remain restricted.

## 7. Mark Ready

Ready means the work is finished for collection. It is not the same as paid or handed over.

## 8. Deliver

Use the authorized collection action, confirm payment or permitted credit and hand over the device. The repair becomes Delivered.

## 9. Find and reprint

Authorized Admin users open Receipts and search a retail or repair invoice. Repair-family rows stay separately printable; an exact invoice match is shown first. Reprinting does not create another sale. Paper-use records are estimates, not printer-success receipts.

## 10. Check the business

Use Dashboard and Reports to compare invoiced amounts, payments collected and outstanding balances. The Owner's Billing & Usage is the Orbito service account, not your Shop's sales total.

If a feature is missing, ask about role and plan settings. Keep individual accounts, deactivate departing staff, do not share approval PINs, and use Help & Privacy for the full guide. Normal transactions require connectivity.
